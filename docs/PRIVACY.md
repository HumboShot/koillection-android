# Privacy policy

This policy applies to the Android app in this repository, an unofficial client for [Koillection](https://github.com/benjaminjonard/koillection).

## Summary

The app does not collect, sell or share your data. The developer does not receive any of your data.

## What the app sends, and to whom

- The app connects only to the Koillection server that you enter on the login screen.
- It sends your username and password to that server to log in.
- It loads and changes your collections, items and other data on that server when you use the app.
- The operator of that server controls this data. Their own privacy policy applies to it.

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting service. It does not connect to any other server.

## What the app stores on your device

- The server address, your username, your password and the login token.
- These values are encrypted with keys in the Android Keystore. The keys cannot leave the device.
- When "Unlock with fingerprint" is on, the password can only be decrypted after your fingerprint or screen lock.
- The login data is not included in Android backups or device transfers.
- The app keeps the last server address and the app settings after you log out, so the login screen can fill in the server.
- Images from your server are cached on the device to load faster.

To remove all of this data, log out and uninstall the app, or clear the app data in the Android settings.

## Permissions

- Internet access: to connect to your Koillection server.
- Network state: the image loader checks for a connection before it loads images.
- Fingerprint or screen lock: only when "Unlock with fingerprint" is on.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
